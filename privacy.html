Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

FX Arena ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is an educational forex trading simulation platform operated by GodSpeed Technologies.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use FX Arena.

2. Information We Collect

To provide account, tournament, and learning features, FX Arena may collect:

  • Account information such as username, email address, profile information, and profile images uploaded by users.
  • Authentication information required to create and secure user accounts.
  • Tournament information including participation history, rankings, virtual balances, scores, and performance statistics.
  • User-provided information such as country selection, avatar, or optional profile details.
  • Technical information such as device information, application performance data, and basic usage statistics.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected by FX Arena is used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide tournament participation and leaderboard features.
  • Maintain fair competition and prevent abuse.
  • Improve application performance and user experience.
  • Send important service announcements and updates.

4. Data Storage and Security

FX Arena uses trusted third-party infrastructure providers, including Firebase services, to store and manage user information.

We apply reasonable security measures designed to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

5. Sharing of Information

FX Arena does not sell, rent, or trade personal user information.

Information may only be shared when necessary:

  • To provide required app functionality through trusted service providers.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the platform.
  • To investigate fraud, abuse, or violations of tournament rules.

6. Simulated Trading Data

FX Arena does not collect real financial trading information because the platform does not execute real trades.

All charts, balances, rankings, and trading activities inside the application are simulation data created for educational and competitive purposes.

7. Firebase and Third-Party Services

FX Arena may use third-party services including:

  • Firebase Authentication for account management.
  • Firebase Firestore for storing application data.
  • Firebase Storage for user-uploaded content.
  • Other technical services required for application operation.

These services operate according to their own privacy policies.

8. User Rights

Users may request:

  • Account deletion.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Access to information associated with their account.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The mobile application may collect limited technical information required for performance, security, and service improvement.

FX Arena does not use invasive tracking or sell user information for advertising purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

FX Arena is intended for users who meet the legal age requirements in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the minimum legal age required by applicable laws.

11. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information. Certain records may be retained when required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, please contact GodSpeed Technologies through the official FX Arena support channels.
