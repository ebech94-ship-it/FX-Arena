About GS Mobility

Building a connected transport network, starting with intercity mobility in Cameroon.

GS Mobility is a GodSpeed Technologies Network initiative focused on making passenger transport more accessible and helping transport agencies coordinate their operations through digital tools.

Our planned network brings passenger services and operator management together, creating a foundation for a more connected transport experience.
