Delete Your FX ARENA Account

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

FX ARENA respects your privacy and gives every user the ability to permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from within the FX ARENA app.

  1. Sign in to your FX ARENA account.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Go to Settings.
  4. Tap the red Delete Account button.
  5. Read the confirmation message carefully.
  6. Confirm that you wish to permanently delete your account.

Once confirmed, your deletion request is processed immediately and cannot be undone.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your associated data, including:

  • Your profile information.
  • Your email address and login credentials.
  • Your trading history and performance statistics.
  • Your tournament participation records.
  • Your leaderboard records.
  • Your saved preferences and settings.
  • Any other personal information associated with your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain limited information may be retained only where required to:

  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats.
  • Resolve disputes or enforce our Terms and Conditions.
  • Maintain essential backup or audit records for a limited retention period.

Any retained information is kept only for legitimate legal, security, or regulatory purposes and is not used for marketing.

Need Help?

If you experience any difficulty deleting your account, please contact our support team using the contact information provided within the app or on our website.
